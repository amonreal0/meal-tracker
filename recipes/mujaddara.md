# Mujaddara with crispy onions + chopped salad

Makes **6 servings**. The Syrian/Levantine comfort classic: **lentils and rice under a heap of
deeply browned crispy onions.** Picked as Laurita's first **Syrian food** (she wants to try it),
and it hits two of her loves (lentils and rice). Vegetarian, cheap, and great as a packed lunch.
For him, it's crispy and savory, and the salad plus spicy yogurt give the fresh-and-rich contrast.
**No tahini, no eggplant.**

> Allergy check: single spices only. The Greek yogurt label should be just milk plus cultures.

## Ingredients
### Crispy onions (the whole point)
- **3 large yellow onions**, halved and thinly sliced
- ½ cup olive oil (strain and keep the onion oil after; it's liquid gold for the rice)
- Salt

### Lentils + rice
- **1¼ cups brown or green lentils**, rinsed
- **1 cup long-grain white rice**, rinsed
- 2 tsp ground cumin · 1 tsp salt (more to taste) · ½ tsp black pepper
- Optional, if FD has it: ½ tsp allspice; **Aleppo pepper** to finish

### Spicy yogurt
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt + 1 small garlic clove (grated) + lemon + salt + **a spoon of harissa**

### Chopped salad (fattoush-ish, no pita)
- 1–2 romaine hearts, 1 cucumber, 1 pint grape tomatoes, a handful of parsley, a few red onion
  slivers (optional)
- Lemon + olive oil + salt. **Sumac** if FD has it (lemony, very Syrian). Pickled-jalapeño
  brine works too, if he wants it.

## Method
1. **Onions (~25 min, mostly hands-off):** in a big skillet, heat the olive oil over
   medium-high. Add the onions with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring every few minutes, until
   **deep brown and crisp at the edges**. Lower the heat as they darken so they don't burn.
   Lift out half onto paper towels (these crisp up as the topping). Leave the rest soft and
   jammy in the pan.
2. **Lentils:** meanwhile, simmer the lentils in plenty of salted water **~15 min**, until just
   tender but still holding shape. Drain, keeping ~2½ cups of the cooking liquid.
3. **Combine:** to the jammy onions in the pan, add the rice, cumin and pepper and toast 1 min.
   Add the lentils plus **2 cups of the lentil liquid** and 1 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, cover,
   and cook on low **18 min**. Rest 10 min covered, then fluff.
4. **Yogurt and salad** while the rice cooks. Dress the salad at the last minute.
5. Serve: mujaddara → heap of crispy onions → spicy yogurt → salad alongside. Aleppo or pepper
   flakes on top.

**Leftover move:** keeps 4–5 days and is good warm or room-temp, which makes it perfect for
lunches. Store the crispy onions separately so they stay crisp. Dress the salad per serving.
