# Pan-seared branzino with tomato-cucumber salad

Makes **2 servings**. Born from the week of 2026-08-24: FD's top-rated branzino fillet + the
"lighter, no breading" correction after the shrimp-bowl miss. The crisp comes from the **skin**,
not a coating — hot pan, dry fish, patience. **Cook-and-eat night** (fish is best day-of;
cooked fish reheats poorly).

## Ingredients

### Fish
- 2 branzino fillets, skin-on (~¾–1 lb total), patted VERY dry
- Salt + pepper; optional light dust of garlic powder *(on hand)*
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil *(on hand)*
- 1 lemon (half for the pan/serving, half for the salad)
- 1–2 garlic cloves, smashed (perfume the oil at the end — don't burn them)

### Tomato-cucumber salad
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 1 cup cherry/grape tomatoes, halved
- A little thin-sliced red onion
- Olive oil, lemon juice (or pickled-jalapeño brine), salt + pepper
- Optional: pickled jalapeños, a few chili flakes

### Optional starch (keep it light — the 08-18 shrimp bowl drowned in rice)
- Small portion of couscous *(on hand)*, or skip entirely — the salad carries it

## Method
1. Mix the salad; let it sit while you cook.
2. Dry the fillets again. Score the skin lightly if it's arching. Season.
3. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Fillets in **skin-side down**,
   press flat with a spatula for the first 30 sec. Leave alone ~3–4 min until the skin is
   crisp and the flesh is opaque ⅔ up the side.
4. Flip for 1–2 min. Kill the heat, add the smashed garlic + a squeeze of lemon to the pan,
   spoon it over.
5. Plate skin-side up next to the salad. Lemon wedges over everything.

**Leftover move:** none — buy only what you'll eat that night. Flaked cold leftovers go on
tomorrow's salad, not reheated.
