# Steak salad with chimichurri

Makes **2 servings**. A big salad that's actually dinner — seared steak sliced over greens
with a punchy garlic-parsley-chili chimichurri that doubles as the dressing. Fresh + rich,
acid-forward, no feta.

## Ingredients

### Chimichurri
- 1 cup parsley, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes (or 1 fresh chile, minced)
- ⅓ cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar — OR pickled jalapeño brine (his preferred acid)
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp salt

### Salad
- ~¾ lb steak (skirt, flank, sirloin, or ribeye)
- Salt + pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil for searing
- ~5 oz arugula (or arugula/spinach mix)
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- ~1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ avocado, sliced
- Pickled jalapeños (optional, extra kick)

## Method
1. **Chimichurri:** stir everything together; let it sit while you cook so the garlic mellows.
2. Pat steak dry, salt + pepper generously.
3. Sear in a hot skillet, 3–4 min per side for medium-rare (adjust to thickness). Rest 5–10 min.
4. Toss greens, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion with a spoonful of chimichurri (or a little
   jalapeño brine + olive oil).
5. Slice steak **against the grain**. Lay over the salad with the avocado.
6. Spoon chimichurri over the steak. Top with pickled jalapeños if you want more heat.

**Leftover move:** chimichurri keeps a week in the fridge — great on eggs, chicken, or a
sandwich. Cook a little extra steak and it becomes a steak torta or rice bowl the next day.
