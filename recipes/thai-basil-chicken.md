# Thai basil chicken over rice (pad krapow gai)

Makes **2 servings**. ~20 minutes, high-satisfaction. Ground chicken (or pork) ripped
through garlic and chile, salty-savory sauce, lots of basil, crispy fried egg on top.

## Ingredients

### Stir-fry
- 1 lb ground chicken (or pork)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2–4 Thai chiles (or 1 serrano/jalapeño + red pepper flakes), chopped
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- ½ small onion or a few shallots, sliced (optional)
- Big handful Thai basil leaves (regular basil works in a pinch)

### Sauce (stir together)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or +1 tsp soy if you don't have it)
- 1 tsp fish sauce (optional but classic)
- ¼ tsp sugar (balances the salt — not for sweetness; skippable)
- Splash of water

### To serve
- Cooked jasmine rice
- 2 fried eggs (crispy edges)
- Lime wedges, extra chili

## Method
1. Start the rice. Mix the sauce in a small bowl.
2. Fry the eggs in hot oil until edges are crispy; set aside.
3. In the same pan/wok, heat oil over high. Add garlic + chiles, ~20 sec.
4. Add ground chicken; break up and cook until no longer pink, letting some bits brown.
5. Add onion/shallot if using, cook 1 min.
6. Pour in the sauce; toss until it mostly cooks off and coats the meat, 1–2 min.
7. Kill the heat, fold in the basil until just wilted.
8. Serve over rice with a crispy fried egg on top, lime on the side.

**Leftover move:** reheats fine; add a fresh fried egg to make it feel new. Rice optional if
you'd rather keep carbs light — it's good in lettuce cups too.
