# Air-fryer chicken cutlet over arugula

Makes **2 servings** (cook a little extra — leftover cutlet is great cold in a sandwich or
chopped onto a salad). Milanese-style: pounded-thin chicken, crunchy panko crust from the air
fryer, a big pile of lemony arugula on top so the greens wilt slightly against the hot cutlet.
**Crispy + fresh + acidic** — a summer-light take on the fried itch.

## Ingredients

### Cutlets
- ~¾–1 lb chicken breast, sliced/pounded into thin cutlets (or buy thin-sliced cutlets)
- ⅓ cup flour *(or cornstarch — on hand)*
- 1 egg, beaten
- ¾ cup panko *(on hand, partial — rebuy)*
- Optional: 2 tbsp grated parmesan in the panko
- Salt, pepper, ½ tsp garlic powder *(on hand)*
- Oil spray for the air-fryer basket

### Arugula topper
- Big handful **arugula** per plate *(shared clamshell with the chickpea salad)*
- Juice of ½ lemon + 1 tbsp olive oil *(on hand)* + a splash of pickled-jalapeño brine, salt
- Optional: thin red onion, cherry tomatoes, shaved parmesan

## Method
1. **Bread:** season cutlets. Set up flour → beaten egg → panko (+ parmesan). Dredge each cutlet
   flour → egg → panko, pressing to coat.
2. **Air-fry:** spray the basket and the tops of the cutlets. Air-fry ~10–12 min at 400°F,
   flipping once, until deep golden and cooked through (165°F). Don't crowd — do in batches if
   needed. Salt out of the basket.
3. **Dress the arugula** right before serving: toss with lemon, olive oil, brine, salt.
4. **Plate:** cutlet down, big pile of dressed arugula on top, extra lemon. The greens wilt a
   little against the hot crust — that's the point.

**Leftover move:** cold cutlet in a sandwich (bread + spicy mayo + arugula) or sliced onto the
crispy chickpea salad.
