# Guiso de garbanzo cubano

Makes **6 servings**: two dinners for two plus Laurita's lunches. **Laurita's #1 dish**, her
Cuban grandmother's, which she called "the most nostalgic thing in the world." Her grandma's
version, per Laurita (2026-10-01): **chorizo, potatoes, thick (not soupy), over white rice.**

> ⚠️ **This one is about matching a memory.** The method below is a classic Cuban guiso built
> from her four details. After the first make, **ask Laurita what was different from Grandma's**
> and log it. Expect it to take a couple of tries.

> ⚠️ **Allergy check:** the **chorizo label must list every spice.** "Spices" alone isn't good
> enough, and neither is anything that mentions mustard. The crushed tomatoes and canned
> chickpeas also need clean labels (no "spices"/"natural flavors"). Not the Italian medley,
> which contains mustard.

## Ingredients
- **6–8 oz Spanish-style cured chorizo** (verified label), sliced into half-moons
- 3 cans (15 oz) **chickpeas**, drained and rinsed
- **1½ lb Yukon gold potatoes**, ¾" dice
- **Sofrito:** 1 large onion (diced), 1 green bell pepper (diced), 5 garlic cloves (minced)
- 1 can (15 oz) **crushed tomatoes** (or tomato sauce)
- 1 tsp ground cumin · 1 tsp dried oregano (single herb) · 1 tsp smoked paprika · 2 bay leaves
- ~2 cups **chicken broth** (label-checked) or water
- 2 tbsp olive oil · salt · black pepper
- Heat for him (and Laurita, who rates herself 5/5): pickled jalapeños or crushed árbol at the table
- To serve: **white rice** (long-grain), parsley

## Method
1. **Render the chorizo:** in a heavy pot over medium, cook the chorizo in the olive oil ~4 min
   until it releases its red fat. Keep it in the pot.
2. **Sofrito:** add onion and green pepper; cook ~8 min until soft and sweet. Add garlic, cumin,
   oregano and paprika; cook 1 min.
3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and cook 3–4 min to deepen.
4. Add potatoes, chickpeas, bay leaves and ~2 cups broth, just enough to *barely* cover.
   Bring to a boil, then **simmer partly covered ~25–30 min** until the potatoes are tender.
5. **Thicken:** uncover and simmer 5–10 min more. Mash a ladleful of chickpeas and potatoes
   against the pot side and stir it back in. Target: **thick and spoonable, not soupy.**
6. Pull the bay leaves. Taste for salt (chorizo is salty, so salt at the end). Serve over white
   rice with parsley.

**Leftover move:** it gets better on day 2–3. Reheat with a splash of water. Pack Laurita's
lunches over rice. Keeps 4–5 days.

**Rice:** make ~2 cups dry long-grain rice for the batch (≈6 cups cooked). Rice is the point
for her. He can take a smaller scoop.
