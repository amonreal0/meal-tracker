# Mojo chicken thighs with air-fryer tostones

Makes **4 servings of chicken** (two dinners, or one dinner plus Laurita's lunches). Tostones
are **best fresh**, so make them for dinner only. Cuban *mojo* is a punchy garlic-citrus-cumin
marinade, savory and sour, not sweet. **Tostones are one of Laurita's loves.** For him, the
tostones are the starch, so skip the rice; she can have rice on the side.

> Allergy check: everything here is single-ingredient. Use plain dried oregano, not the
> Italian medley (which has mustard). No bottled mojo: those list "spices."

## Ingredients
### Mojo (makes ~1 cup; reserve ¼ cup *before* it touches raw chicken, as the tostones dip)
- ½ cup fresh **orange juice** (~2 oranges) + ¼ cup fresh **lime juice** (~3 limes). That's the
  standard stand-in for *naranja agria* (sour orange).
- **8 garlic cloves**, smashed and minced with a pinch of salt
- ⅓ cup olive oil · 1 tsp ground cumin · 1 tsp dried oregano · 1 tsp salt · ½ tsp pepper
- Heat: crushed árbol or red pepper flakes (they're both 5/5 heat people)

### Chicken
- **2½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs** (pasture-raised)

### Tostones
- **3 green plantains** (fully green and firm, *not* yellow, which would be maduros)
- Neutral oil spray or 1–2 tbsp oil · flaky/kosher salt
- To serve: the reserved mojo for dipping, lime wedges, sliced onion, avocado

## Method
1. **Marinate:** whisk the mojo. Set ¼ cup aside for the dip. Pour the rest over the thighs.
   Marinate **at least 1 hr, ideally overnight** (not more than ~24 hr; the citrus turns it
   mushy).
2. **Tostones, round 1:** peel the plantains (cut off the ends, slit the skin lengthwise, pry it
   off) and cut into 1" chunks. Toss with oil and air-fry at **400°F for 8 min**, until tender
   but pale.
3. **Smash:** press each chunk flat to ~¼" with the bottom of a glass or a mug. Brush or spray
   with oil.
4. **Chicken (while the tostones finish):** pull the thighs from the marinade and pat them
   *dry*. Sear in a hot oiled skillet ~5–6 min per side, until charred at the edges and 175°F
   inside. (Or broil.) Rest 5 min and slice.
   **Pan sauce (optional, ~4 min):** pour off excess fat, add the leftover marinade + a splash
   of broth or water to the same skillet, and scrape up the browned bits. Bring it to a **full
   rolling boil for at least 1 min** (it touched raw chicken), then reduce 2–3 min until it
   coats a spoon. Off heat: fresh lime squeeze, taste for salt. Spoon over the sliced chicken.
   Different from the reserved raw mojo: cooked = mellow and savory, raw = sharp dip.
5. **Tostones, round 2:** air-fry at **400°F for 8–10 min**, flipping once, until golden and
   crisp. Salt *immediately*.
6. Plate: chicken + tostones + reserved mojo to dip + lime + raw onion slivers.

**Leftover move:** the chicken reheats well. Slice it for Laurita's lunch over rice, or into a
sandwich or bowl. Don't save the tostones.
