# Crispy chickpea salad with hummus-lemon dressing

Makes **2 servings** as a real dinner. A ⭐ standout. The original is a tahini-lemon dressing,
but **he's not a tahini fan** — so this uses a **hummus-based dressing** instead: same creamy,
nutty body, none of the assertive raw-tahini bitterness (hummus is chickpeas + a little tahini +
lemon + garlic, already balanced). Best eaten right after the chickpeas come out of the air fryer,
while they're still crunchy.

## Ingredients

### Crispy chickpeas
- 1 can (15.5 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and **rolled dry in a towel** (dry = crispy)
- 1 tbsp olive oil *(on hand)*
- ½ tsp ground cumin *(on hand)*, ½ tsp smoked paprika *(on hand)*, salt

### Hummus-lemon dressing (his swap for tahini)
- 3 tbsp hummus
- Juice of ½–1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- A splash of **pickled-jalapeño brine** *(on hand — his salad acid)* + a few chopped jalapeños
- Water, a teaspoon at a time, to thin to a drizzle
- Salt + pepper, small grate of garlic *(on hand)*

### Salad
- Big base of **arugula** *(shared clamshell with the chicken cutlet this week)*
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- ~1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- Thin-sliced red onion (or the on-hand onion)
- ½–1 avocado, sliced
- **No feta** — he dislikes it; the crispy chickpeas + avocado carry the richness

## Method
1. **Chickpeas:** toss dried chickpeas with oil, cumin, paprika, salt. Air-fry ~15 min at 400°F,
   shaking once, until crunchy. Salt again straight out of the basket.
2. **Dressing:** whisk hummus, lemon, olive oil, jalapeño brine, garlic, salt + pepper. Thin with
   water to a drizzle.
3. **Build:** arugula → cucumber, tomato, onion → warm crispy chickpeas on top → drizzle dressing →
   avocado → extra jalapeños. Eat while the chickpeas are still crunchy.

**Notes:** the chickpeas + air fryer share the same gear/rhythm as the chicken cutlet night. If
you want it heartier, chopped leftover chicken cutlet on top is great (skip the jammy egg / feta —
not his thing).
