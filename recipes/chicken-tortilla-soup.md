# Chicken tortilla soup

Makes **2 large servings** (cook-once-eat-twice). Brothy, not a heavy braise — summer-OK.
The toppings are the point: keep them crisp/fresh and add at the bowl, not the pot.

## Ingredients

### Soup
- 1 tbsp neutral or olive oil
- ½ onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1–2 chipotles in adobo, chopped + 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 can (14–15 oz) crushed or fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 3–4 cups chicken broth (or water + Better Than Bouillon)
- ~¾–1 lb boneless chicken thighs or breast (or use rotisserie/leftover chicken)
- Salt, to taste
- Lime juice, to finish

### Toppings (at the bowl)
- 2–3 corn tortillas (Vista Hermosa), cut in strips + fried/air-fried crisp — OR store tortilla chips
- ½ avocado, diced
- Pickled jalapeños
- Chopped parsley (or skip)
- Lime wedges
- Optional: shredded cheese (parm/mozz/jack — no feta)

## Method
1. Heat oil in a pot. Sauté onion 3–4 min until soft.
2. Add garlic, chipotle + adobo, cumin, oregano. Cook 30 sec.
3. Add tomatoes and broth. Bring to a simmer.
4. Slip in the raw chicken; simmer 15–18 min until cooked through. (If using
   pre-cooked/rotisserie chicken, skip this — just simmer the broth 10 min and add shredded
   chicken at the end to warm.)
5. Pull the chicken out, shred with two forks, return to the pot. Salt to taste; finish with lime.
6. Meanwhile, crisp the tortilla strips: air-fryer ~400°F 5–7 min tossed with a little oil +
   salt (or shallow-fry in a skillet).
7. Ladle into bowls; pile on tortilla strips, avocado, pickled jalapeños, parsley, lime.

**Leftover move:** the broth keeps well; store tortilla strips separately so they stay crisp.
Reheat and re-top fresh. Also good spooned over rice if you want it heartier.
