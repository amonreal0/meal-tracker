# Picadillo with Jalapa Jar salsa

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef (80% lean — same buy as albóndigas)
- 1–2 **yukon gold or russet potatoes**, small dice (~½") — the upgrade he called for (2026-08-11)
- ½ onion, chopped
- 2–3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp oregano, salt + pepper
- ~½–1 cup **Jalapa Jar fresh salsa**, stirred in (the move that made it)
- Optional finishers: pickled jalapeños, lime, avocado
- To serve: warm corn tortillas (Vista Hermosa), or over rice, or just a bowl

## Method (reconstructed — correct me)
1. **Potatoes first:** brown the diced potatoes in oil until golden and nearly tender (~8–10 min),
   or par-cook 4 min in the microwave then crisp. Set aside. (Classic picadillo simmers them in
   the sauce — browning first keeps them from going mushy.)
2. Brown the beef in the same hot skillet, breaking it up; pour off excess fat if needed.
3. Add onion, cook until soft; add garlic + spices, 1 min.
4. Stir in the salsa and the potatoes; simmer 5–10 min until it thickens into a saucy,
   taco-filling texture and the potatoes are tender.
5. Taste for salt; finish with lime. Eat in tortillas / over rice / as a bowl with avocado.

**Leftover move:** classic cook-once-eat-twice — reheats great; second night as tacos or over
rice with a fried egg.
